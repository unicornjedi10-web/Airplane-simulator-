Cockpit Controls Information

Altitude Indicator

Shows your current altitude above ground level in feet.

Airspeed Indicator

Displays your current speed in knots. Keep within safe operating limits.

Artificial Horizon

Indicates your aircraft's pitch and roll relative to the horizon.

Heading Indicator

Shows your current compass heading (0-360 degrees).

Vertical Speed Indicator

Shows rate of climb or descent in feet per minute.

Engine Power

Throttle control from 0% to 100%. More power increases speed.

Landing Gear

Deploy landing gear before landing. Retract after takeoff.

Flaps

Deploy flaps to reduce speed and increase lift during landing.

Radar

Shows nearby terrain and obstacles. Use for navigation.
